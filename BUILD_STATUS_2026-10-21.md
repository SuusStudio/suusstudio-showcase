## Evidence and limits

- **STEM V1.4.4:** a **derived-data** robustness experiment; **11/20** correct known-image acceptances and **30/30** unknown-derived images withheld. The images share sources with earlier tests. These outcomes do not demonstrate performance on an independent real-world distribution.
- **STEM autonomy:** limited synthetic task runs and internal engineering tests demonstrate selected functionality and blocked invalid proposals. They do **not** establish self-directed general intelligence or customer-safe autonomous execution.
- **Visual simulations:** technical rendering success and creative/visual approval are different milestones.
- **Internal test counts** apply only to the named version and the defined test conditions. They are not outside certification, uptime guarantees, cybersecurity certifications, clinical validation or commercial acceptance.
- **AGI, implantable interfaces, full-body control and unrestricted autonomous agents are not released or demonstrated here.** Harmful fictional technologies remain non-operational concepts.

## What remains private

The studio deliberately withholds proprietary prompts, underlying code, security rules, internals of agent coordination, routing and model selection, raw benchmarks and datasets, research media, private customer material, business strategy, account identifiers, credentials, detailed infrastructure and protected creative methods.

The [STEM visual showcase](./stem/index.html) uses concept artwork and explanatory interactions, not an exposed live agent. [The Framework](./FRAMEWORK.md) describes principles, not the engine.

## Publication standard

**Show the capability. Identify the evidence boundary. Protect the invention.**

Every public status needs a corresponding date and scope. **LAB PASS** does not mean **PRODUCTION PASS**. Independent review and the founder's explicit human release are separate gates.
