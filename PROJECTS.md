# Selected Project Areas

This page gives a high-level view of the kind of work developed under SuusStudio™ without publishing proprietary implementation details.

## AI Creative Operating Systems
Reusable structures that connect creative direction, prompts, consistency rules, quality review and final delivery.

**Focus:** repeatability, consistency, quality control, creative direction.

## Visual QA Frameworks
Evaluation systems for AI-assisted visual work.

**Focus:** identity consistency, physical realism, anatomy, lighting, scene coherence, typography and presentation quality.

## Creator Provenance Concepts
Systems and prototypes for documenting asset origin, approvals, usage scope and creator rights.

**Focus:** provenance, consent, traceability and responsible deployment.

## Web & Interactive Prototypes
Custom websites, dashboards and lightweight applications designed around a specific workflow rather than generic templates.

**Focus:** React/Vite front ends, Node/Express prototypes, structured data and Vercel deployments.

## Workflow Automation
Operational tools that reduce repetitive work while keeping important actions reviewable.

**Focus:** structured workflows, auditability, fail-safe behavior and human control.

## Campaign Systems
Creative systems that combine storytelling, marketing and production structure.

**Focus:** concepts that can scale from a single visual into a repeatable campaign system.

---

Detailed implementation, private repositories and commercial source code are available only where appropriate under a defined project scope.
